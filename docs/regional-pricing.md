# Fixed regional pricing catalog

`backend/src/bridge/shared/regionalPricing.js` is the single price catalog for the API and Cloudflare Worker. `pricingForCountry(code)` returns the charge currency, Stripe `unit_amount` integers, regional free-account limit, and a `usesFallback` flag. A recognized Sub-Saharan African country gets the regional catalog (2 free accounts); other or unknown countries get the global USD catalog (5 free accounts). The SSA annual charge is exactly 10 monthly charges, a 16.67% discount from 12 months. Global USD charges remain $29/$39/$99 monthly and $288/$396/$996 yearly.

The starting SSA monthly prices are 5,000/10,000/23,000 XAF. We converted using the [official EUR peg of 655.957 XAF](https://www.beac.int/faqs) (also [655.957 XOF](https://www.bceao.int/fr/content/histoire-du-franc-cfa)) and the 2026-10-09 EUR exchange rates from [Frankfurter v2](https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/rates?base=EUR&expand=providers). The frozen [rate and price snapshot](../backend/src/bridge/shared/regionalPricingSnapshot.json) records each currency's observation date, EUR rate, provider keys, rounding step, and approved Stripe amounts. All 39 mapped currencies had a 2026-10-09 observation. Rates are an input to editorial prices, not a checkout-time conversion. Frankfurter [notes](https://frankfurter.dev/license/) that source providers may have separate terms and that rates can arrive late or change; review the catalog before a future pricing revision.

For each currency, we rounded all three tier equivalents to a common, human-readable step based on its starter equivalent: 0.5 below 10, 1 below 100, 5 below 500, 10 below 1,000, 50 below 5,000, 100 below 20,000, 500 below 100,000, and 1,000 thereafter. We fixed XAF and XOF at the stated reference prices; set EUR to 7.50/15/35 and GBP/SHP to 6.50/13/30. Yearly amounts are 10 times the rounded monthly amount. These values are deliberately static so displayed and billed amounts agree throughout a billing cycle.

We checked native charge support against [Stripe's presentment currency list](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies). ERN, GHS, MRU, SSP, STN, and ZWG are absent, so their countries use the labeled EUR fallback of €7.50/€15/€35 monthly. The 33 remaining mapped currencies have native catalog prices. Actual currency availability can also depend on the Stripe account country and payment method; confirm those settings before enabling a payment method. Prices exceed Stripe's general converted minimum, though Stripe assesses the minimum in the settlement currency.

Meadow's merchant account is registered in the United States. The native catalog currencies appear in [Stripe's US card-presentment list](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies?presentment-currency=US). Checkout requests above Stripe's general eight-digit non-card amount limit use cards only; Stripe validates the final currency and amount before a session is created. A failed validation clears the pending attempt and returns a clear service error without charging. The browser sends its displayed quote fields with checkout; if the trusted current-IP quote changed, checkout returns `pricing_changed` before contacting Stripe. An existing subscription retains its original currency and amount when the subscriber's location changes.

`monthlyMinor` and `yearlyMinor` are already Stripe charge amounts. BIF, DJF, GNF, KMF, MGA, RWF, XAF, and XOF use zero-decimal amounts and `currencyExponent: 0`. Other currencies use `currencyExponent: 2`. UGX is a [Stripe exception](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies#special-cases): prices are whole shillings but Stripe requires the API amount multiplied by 100, with trailing `00`; its `currencyExponent` is therefore 2 for formatting the returned charge amount. The UGX annual pro amount is 160,000,000 minor units; Stripe's documented card limit accommodates it, while some non-card payment methods have an eight-digit limit.
