# Fixed regional pricing catalog

`backend/src/bridge/shared/regionalPricing.js` is the single price catalog for the API and Cloudflare Worker. `pricingForCountry(code)` returns the charge currency, Stripe `unit_amount` integers, regional free-account limit, and a `usesFallback` flag. Regional pricing is currently limited to Central and West Africa (2 free accounts); other or unknown countries, including Eastern and Southern Africa, get the global USD catalog (5 free accounts). The regional annual charge is exactly 10 monthly charges, a 16.67% discount from 12 months. Global USD charges remain $29/$39/$99 monthly and $288/$396/$996 yearly.

The eligibility list in `backend/src/bridge/shared/geography.js` follows [UN M49 Middle and Western Africa](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/):

- Central Africa: Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, and São Tomé and Príncipe.
- West Africa: Benin, Burkina Faso, Cabo Verde, Côte d’Ivoire, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Mauritania, Niger, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone, and Togo.
- Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha (`SH`) remains eligible as a Western African territory.

The public quote, new checkout, and regional Free account limit share this boundary. Existing subscriptions keep their saved currency and amount even if their country is no longer eligible. The API and saved billing records retain the `ssa` region identifier for compatibility; it is not the eligibility definition.

The starting regional monthly prices are 5,000/10,000/23,000 XAF. We converted using the [official EUR peg of 655.957 XAF](https://www.beac.int/faqs) (also [655.957 XOF](https://www.bceao.int/fr/content/histoire-du-franc-cfa)) and the 2026-10-09 EUR exchange rates from [Frankfurter v2](https://api.frankfurter.dev/v2/rates?base=EUR&expand=providers). The frozen [rate and price snapshot](../backend/src/bridge/shared/regionalPricingSnapshot.json) records each currency's observation date, EUR rate, provider keys, rounding step, and approved Stripe amounts. The original, broader Sub-Saharan snapshot covers 39 currencies with a 2026-10-09 observation; changing country eligibility does not revise these frozen prices. Rates are an input to editorial prices, not a checkout-time conversion. Frankfurter [notes](https://frankfurter.dev/license/) that source providers may have separate terms and that rates can arrive late or change; review the catalog before a future pricing revision.

For each currency, we rounded all three tier equivalents to a common, human-readable step based on its starter equivalent: 0.5 below 10, 1 below 100, 5 below 500, 10 below 1,000, 50 below 5,000, 100 below 20,000, 500 below 100,000, and 1,000 thereafter. We fixed XAF and XOF at the stated reference prices; set EUR to 7.50/15/35 and GBP/SHP to 6.50/13/30. Yearly amounts are 10 times the rounded monthly amount. These values are deliberately static so displayed and billed amounts agree throughout a billing cycle.

We checked native charge support against [Stripe's presentment currency list](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies). The current eligibility list maps to 14 native currencies: 11 have native catalog prices, while Ghana (`GHS`), Mauritania (`MRU`), and São Tomé and Príncipe (`STN`) use the labeled EUR fallback of €7.50/€15/€35 monthly. The original snapshot also records unsupported ERN, SSP, and ZWG; those countries now receive global USD quotes. Actual currency availability can also depend on the Stripe account country and payment method; confirm those settings before enabling a payment method. Prices exceed Stripe's general converted minimum, though Stripe assesses the minimum in the settlement currency.

Meadow's merchant account is registered in the United States. The native catalog currencies appear in [Stripe's US card-presentment list](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies?presentment-currency=US). Checkout requests above Stripe's general eight-digit non-card amount limit use cards only; Stripe validates the final currency and amount before a session is created. A failed validation clears the pending attempt and returns a clear service error without charging. The browser sends its displayed quote fields with checkout; if the trusted current-IP quote changed, checkout returns `pricing_changed` before contacting Stripe. An existing subscription retains its original currency and amount when the subscriber's location changes.

`monthlyMinor` and `yearlyMinor` are already Stripe charge amounts. Currently eligible GNF, XAF, and XOF use zero-decimal amounts and `currencyExponent: 0`; other current quote currencies use `currencyExponent: 2`. The original catalog also contains zero-decimal BIF, DJF, KMF, MGA, and RWF. Its historical UGX prices follow the [Stripe exception](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies#special-cases): prices are whole shillings but the API amount is multiplied by 100, with trailing `00`. The saved UGX annual pro amount is 160,000,000 minor units; narrowing eligibility does not change an existing subscription's amount.
